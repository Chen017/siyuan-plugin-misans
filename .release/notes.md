MiSans Regular and Bold for the SiYuan interface and editor.

**Requires SiYuan 3.8.4 or later.**

Download `package.zip`, extract it to `data/plugins/siyuan-plugin-misans/`, and enable the plugin in SiYuan.

Based on [TCOTC's font plugin](https://github.com/TCOTC/siyuan-ttf-HarmonyOS_Sans_SC-and-Twemoji). MiSans by Xiaomi and its font partners.
